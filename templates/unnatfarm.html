The Challenge

Barriers Limiting Farmer Growth and Sustainability

Today's Agri-Allied (Fisheries& Livestock) Farming Reality

1

In Assam and the North East, fisheries & livestock farming are preferred livelihood choices due to: Strong cultural preference for fish meat, eggs, & milk Small and fragmented landholdings.. Faster and more regular income cycles compared to crops Though the consumption is high; there are still gaps in production of all the livestocks due to uonrganized value chains.

2

Farmers today operate in an increasingly uncertain environment marked by: Rising input costs and irregular availability Climate variability, floods, and unpredictable weather Dependence on informal markets and middlemen Information asymmetry on prices, technologies, schemes

3

Farmers are interested, but timely and quality advisory services are lacking. Lack of guidance to reach potential vendors that can supply quality inputs Inadequate veterinary support at the village level Lack of awareness on disease prevalence and prevention Weak price realization due to trader’s monopoly and unorganized market linkages

Solutions for Every Stage

Planning & Preparation

Farm design and construction that support seasonal management, enable readiness to kickstart farming operations & improve existing husbandry practices for higher yields.

Market Access

Direct connections to buyers, price transparency, logistics support, and payment security.

Financial Management

Mobilization of Finance, Expense tracking, optimizing COGS, profit analysis, credit facilitation, and financial planning tools.

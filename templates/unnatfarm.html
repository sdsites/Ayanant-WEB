The Challenge

Barriers Limiting Farmer Growth and Sustainability

Today's Agri-Allied (Fisheries& Livestock) Farming Reality

In Assam and the North East, fisheries & livestock farming are preferred livelihood choices due to:

  • Strong cultural preference for fish, meat, eggs, & milk
  • Small and fragmented landholdings..
  • Faster and more regular income cycles compared to crops
Though the consumption is high; there are still gaps in production of all the livestocks due to uonrganized value chains.

Farmers today operate in an increasingly uncertain environment marked by:

  • Rising input costs and irregular availability
  • Climate variability, floods, and unpredictable weather
  • Dependence on informal markets and middlemen
  • Information asymmetry on prices, technologies, schemes

Farmers are interested, but timely and quality advisory services are lacking.

  • Lack of guidance to reach potential vendors that can supply quality inputs
  • Inadequate veterinary support at the village level
  • Lack of awareness on disease prevalence and prevention
  • Weak price realization due to trader’s monopoly and unorganized market linkages

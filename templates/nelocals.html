What Is NE LOCALS?

Bringing Northeast India to the World

NE LOCALS is a market access and brand-building initiative dedicated to bringing the authentic products, traditions, and craftsmanship of Northeast India to consumers across the country.

Authentic Products

Traditional foods, indigenous textiles, handicrafts, GI-tagged products, and local specialties from Northeast India.

Trusted Producers

Direct sourcing from farmers, FPCs, SHGs, artisans, women entrepreneurs, and rural producer groups.

Sustainable Impact

Every purchase supports rural livelihoods, local enterprises, fair returns, and community-led development.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Promote authentic regional products

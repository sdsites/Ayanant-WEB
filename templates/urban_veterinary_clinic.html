Meet Dr. Ranjan Neog

Meet Dr. Ranjan Neog

Dr. Ranjan Neog is an experienced veterinarian with extensive practical expertise in the preventive healthcare, treatment and management of pet animals as well as other livestock species. His approach combines professional knowledge, hands-on experience and genuine compassion for animals.

At Urban Veterinary Clinic, Guwahati, Dr. Neog is supported by a dedicated team that assists in day-to-day clinical operations, patient care and overall service delivery. Together, they strive to provide thoughtful diagnosis, appropriate treatment, preventive healthcare and clear communication with the pet owners.

The services we provide

What We Offer

Solutions that transform ideas into thriving enterprises.

Compliance support

We provide end-to-end compliance solutions for FPCs, SHGs, and enterprises, ensuring smooth registration, licensing, and statutory adherence for sustainable business operations.

Agri Trading

Ayanant facilitates market linkages and bulk trading of quality agri-produce, connecting farmer collectives directly with buyers for fair and transparent trade.

Packaging Machinery

We offer cost-effective and efficient packaging machinery solutions tailored for small enterprises and producer groups to enhance product quality and shelf appeal.

Design Support

Our creative team designs impactful packaging and branding solutions that reflect local identity while meeting modern market standards.

Packaging Materials

We supply high-quality, food-grade packaging materials customized for diverse products, ensuring durability, hygiene, and brand consistency.

Training and Capacity Building

Ayanant conducts specialized training programs on business management, marketing, and packaging to strengthen the capacities of rural entrepreneurs and producer groups.

FOR ENTREPRENEURS

Got a product idea? Let’s make it shelf-ready.

We partner with rural and small entrepreneurs to turn home-grown products into market-ready brands — from first label sketches to compliant packaging and real shelf space.

Step 1

Idea to Label

Turn your home recipe or local product into a brand people recognise and remember.

  • Name, logo & label concepts
  • Packaging format suggestions
  • Storytelling rooted in your village / cluster
Step 2

Legal & Compliance

Get the basics in place so your product can legally be on shelves and travel further.

  • FSSAI & registration hand-holding
  • Label information & declarations
  • Simple checklists you can actually use
Step 3

Market Pathway

Test, refine, and grow — from pilot batches to NE LOCALS shelves and B2B buyers.

  • Pilot runs & feedback from real customers
  • Placement in NE LOCALS (where relevant)
  • Roadmap for B2B & modern trade
